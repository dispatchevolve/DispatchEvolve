## Conflict Resolution Semantics

- A precedence edge is required only when two selected Candidates may match the same batch and modify at least one same logical Policy.
- `higher|lower|policies` means the higher Candidate wins that shared-Policy competition when both Scenes match.
- Include every and only the shared Policies governed by that edge.
- The precedence graph must be acyclic.
- A multi-Policy Local Candidate is atomic.
- Selected Candidates without a same-Policy competition may execute together at their normal Engine Policy steps.

## Requirements

- Select at least one selectable Local Candidate.
- Never select a hard-conflict pair.
- Propose an exact Composition absent from Complete Composition Attempt Registry.
- Consider the normalized change of every metric in Experiment Metric Catalog.
- Subject to all supplied reward and guardrail requirements, prefer stronger improvement in `$composition_priority_metric`.
- Use `opportunity_intent` only as the upstream reason for the Candidate; use Candidate Performance as measured local evidence.
- Use Pareto References as full-search trade-off evidence and Recent Composition Attempt Feedback as a recent bounded history. Do not treat either block as the other.
- Return precedence edges for every and only every newly introduced overlapping same-Policy competition.
- Do not claim unmeasured combined or full-engine effects.
- The order of IDs in Selected Local Candidate IDs has no priority meaning.
- Do not use `|` or line breaks inside Rationale.
