## Requirements

- Review the supplied Opportunity as one frozen whole. Do not replace its objective or add or remove Policies.
- ACCEPT when the Scene evidence supports headroom in the selected objective, the Trace connects every named Policy to the Improvement Plan, and a multi-Policy set forms one coherent local task.
- REJECT when the selected objective lacks supplied headroom evidence, a named Policy is unsupported or unrelated, the plan contradicts the supplied Trace, or a multi-Policy set does not form one coherent task.
- Do not require pre-evolution proof of Candidate improvement or protected-metric feasibility.
- Do not invent evidence or claim unsupplied causality.
- Keep the reason concise and tied to the supplied evidence.
