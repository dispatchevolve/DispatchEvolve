## Requirements

- Analyze every supplied pair exactly once.
- Refer only to Candidate IDs and Policy paths present in the Context.
- Refer to candidates by their IDs rather than relying only on positional words such as `left` or `right`.
- Use Engine Policy step order to distinguish earlier, later, and repeated operations.
- Treat `UNKNOWN` as unavailable evidence, not as `NONE`.
- Name every Policy materially involved in the relationship, or `NONE` when no specific Policy can be established.
- Explain the possible interaction without claiming an unmeasured combined metric result.
- Use commas inside the affected-Policies field and do not use `|` inside the description.

LEFT_ID|RIGHT_ID|COMMA_SEPARATED_AFFECTED_POLICIES_OR_NONE|CONCRETE_RELATIONSHIP_DESCRIPTION
